## Where It Grows

Black pepper (Piper nigrum) thrives in the lush, humid Western Ghats of Kerala, South India, a tropical haven historically known as the Spice Coast. Here, pepper vines climb high up natural support trees like silver oak and teak, flourishing in mineral-rich soil and nourished by high-altitude monsoon rains. The berries grow in tightly clustered hanging spikes. Harvesting takes place just once a year between December and March, when the berries turn from vibrant green to a warm yellowish-red, signaling peak flavor development and high essential oil density.

## How It Is Dried & Graded

Once hand-harvested by local farming communities, the fresh green pepper spikes are threshed to separate the individual berries. The berries are blanched briefly in hot water to activate natural enzymes that darken the skin, then spread evenly on outdoor drying mats to sun-dry for three to four days. As moisture evaporates, the outer skin shrinks and darkens into its signature deep brownish-black, wrinkled shell.

After sun-drying, the peppercorns undergo rigorous mechanical and optical grading:

- **Size & Density Screening:** Screened by diameter to separate premium bold berries from smaller kernels.
- **Density Testing (Tellicherry Grade):** Only the largest, heaviest berries — left on the vine longest to develop full essential oils — are selected as top-tier Tellicherry Garbled Extra Bold (TGEB).
- **Optical Cleaning:** Thoroughly sifted to remove dust, stems, and light berries, leaving only uniform, high-piperine peppercorns.

## How It Reaches the UK

To preserve fragile volatile oils, our graded peppercorns are packed into moisture-barrier, food-grade sacks right at the estate. Shipped directly from the port of Cochin, the batches travel via maritime routes straight to UK ports. By working directly with long-established farm partnerships, we eliminate multi-tier broker chains. This direct-trade route ensures the pepper reaches our UK facilities quickly, where it is micro-batched into consumer packaging — delivering intense bite, piney warmth, and freshly harvested aroma straight to your kitchen table.
