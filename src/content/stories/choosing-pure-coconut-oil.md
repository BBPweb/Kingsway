In authentic coastal Indian, Sri Lankan, and Caribbean cooking, coconut oil is not merely a cooking medium, it is the primary flavour carrier that defines the entire dish.

## Understanding Extractions

1. **Cold-Pressed Virgin Oil:** Extracted from fresh coconut milk without chemical processing. Retains a sweet, pronounced tropical aroma ideal for finishing dishes and tempering spices.
2. **Roasted Copra Oil:** Extracted from sun-dried coconut kernels. Offers a deeper, nutty, robust scent beloved in traditional South Indian curries and thorans.

## Temperature Discipline

Coconut oil has a moderately high smoke point (around 177°C / 350°F). For delicate tadkas and spice tempering, keep flame levels medium to avoid scorching mustard seeds and curry leaves.
