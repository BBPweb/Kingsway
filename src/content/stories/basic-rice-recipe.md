## Prep & Cooking Times

Soaking Time: 30 mins

Cook Time: 10 mins

Resting Time: 10 mins

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (200g) Kingsway Extra-Long Basmati Rice
- 1.5 cups (360ml) Water
- ½ tsp Fine sea salt
- 1 tbsp Good-quality ghee or neutral oil
- (Optional aromatics) 2 green cardamom pods, 1 small cinnamon stick, 1 bay leaf

## Method

Rinse the rice under cool running water until it runs completely clear, soak in cold water for 20 to 30 minutes, and drain thoroughly. Heat the ghee in a medium saucepan over medium heat, gently fry the optional whole spices for 30 seconds until aromatic, then add the drained rice, salt, and 1.5 cups of water before bringing it to a rapid, rolling boil uncovered. As soon as it boils, reduce the heat to the absolute lowest setting, cover tightly with a lid, and cook undisturbed for 10 minutes. Turn off the heat and let the pot rest covered for an additional 10 minutes to finish steaming — then gently fluff the grains with a fork to serve.
