In the rich alluvial soils of Palakkad and across southern India, Palakkadan Matta rice has been cultivated for centuries. Distinctive for its stout, plump reddish grains, Matta undergoes an ancient parboiling treatment before milling.

## The Parboiling Process

Steaming the paddy while still within the husk drives water-soluble vitamins and minerals from the bran directly into the starchy endosperm. The result is a firmer grain that resists bursting during lengthy simmering.

## Service in Restaurants

Caterers and commercial restaurants favour parboiled Matta because it maintains its plump, individual texture in warming trays without clumping or turning into starch paste.
