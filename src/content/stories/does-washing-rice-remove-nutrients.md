Few kitchen questions provoke as much debate as whether to rinse rice before cooking.

## Why We Wash

During transit and polishing, fine starches rub off the grains. If not rinsed away, these free amylose starches gelatinise rapidly in hot water, causing cooked grains to stick together in clumps.

## The Verdict on Nutrients

In high-quality aged basmati and traditionally parboiled rice varieties, vital minerals and B vitamins are bound deep within the grain endosperm. A gentle 60-second rinse with cool water removes only loose surface dust and free starches, leaving the core nutrition completely intact.
