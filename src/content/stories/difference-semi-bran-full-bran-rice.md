Milling grain is a question of balancing nutritional integrity with kitchen cooking performance. When rice is harvested from the paddy, it arrives enclosed in a tough outer husk.

## De-Husking and the Bran Layer

Once the outer husk is gently removed, the grain retains its nutrient-dense aleurone and pericarp layers (the bran).

- **Full-Bran Rice:** 100% of the bran layer remains intact. The grain possesses a firm chew, higher mineral content, and takes longer to cook.
- **Semi-Bran (Single-Polished):** Lightly brushed to remove approximately 50% of the outer fibrous layer. The grain softens faster during simmering while preserving the distinctive reddish striations and deep earthy taste.
